Our Story

Since 1976, Nasser Air Travel & Shipping Agencies LLC has been a trusted name in the UAE's travel industry, serving individuals, families, and businesses with excellence and reliability. Established with a vision to make travel seamless and accessible, the company quickly became one of the region's most respected travel pioneers.

Since 1976, Nasser Air Travel & Shipping Agencies LLC has been a trusted name in the UAE's travel industry, serving individuals, families, and businesses with excellence and reliability. Established with a vision to make travel seamless and accessible, the company quickly became one of the region's most respected travel pioneers.

As the demand for leisure travel grew, Nasser Air Travel introduced its dedicated holiday division under the brand name Exciting Holidays. For over three decades, Exciting Holidays became synonymous with memorable vacations and personalized holiday experiences for travelers across the region.

In 2009, the brand evolved into Xperienz Holidays — a modern identity that reflects innovation, creativity, and a customer-first approach while preserving the same trusted values and heritage built over decades.

Today, Xperienz Holidays proudly stands as the leisure travel arm of Nasser Air Travel, backed by nearly 50 years of industry expertise. From Asia and Europe to Africa, the Americas, Australia, and beyond, we craft tailor-made holidays, luxury escapes, honeymoon trips, family vacations, group tours, cruises, and adventure experiences for travelers around the world.

We don't just plan holidays — we create experiences that inspire, connect, and last a lifetime.
