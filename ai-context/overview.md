# Project Overview

## Client & Brand
- **Client:** Xperienz Holidays (part of ClickTap agency work)
- **Brand name in UI:** "Xperienz Holidays" (note: Xperienz, not Xperience)
- **Tagline:** "Your Holidays, Your Style"
- **Business:** UAE-based travel agency — 50+ years legacy, GSA for Air Mauritius in UAE
- **Target market:** UAE travellers (Dubai, Abu Dhabi); currency is AED

## What the app is
A public-facing marketing/booking website for a travel agency. Angular frontend only (no backend yet).

## Key brand facts used throughout the UI
- 100,000+ Happy Travellers
- 50+ Years Legacy
- GSA for Air Mauritius in UAE
- 24/7 UAE Support
- 30,000+ Holidays Planned
- 98% Customer Satisfaction

## Services offered
Holiday packages, flight bookings, hotel reservations, visa assistance, cruises, travel insurance, group tours, honeymoon packages, customized international travel, MICE (corporate meetings/incentives/conferences/events).
